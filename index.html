Sarvesh Baskar

AI Researcher

I recently completed my dual degree program at BITS Pilani, earning both a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science and a Master of Science in Physics. My research expertise spans Large Language Models, reinforcement learning, and AI systems, with a particular focus on bridging theoretical foundations with practical applications.

I'm deeply passionate about exploring the frontiers of Planning, RL agents, computer vision, multimodal AI systems, and the emerging field of image and video generation. I'm particularly interested in how we can combine natural language understanding with visual reasoning and reinforcement learning to create more sophisticated AI systems which can make better decisions. My interdisciplinary background in both computer science and physics provides me with a unique perspective on tackling complex AI challenges from multiple angles, and I'm actively seeking opportunities to contribute to cutting-edge research in multimodal AI and computer vision.

During my recent tenure as a Research Assistant at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County, I worked under Dr. Manas Gaur's supervision, collaborating with IBM researchers to extend the "Automatic Thought of Search" framework to complex planning domains. This experience deepened my understanding of AI reasoning capabilities and advanced planning systems. My research in conversational AI led to the development of the CPER framework, which achieved up to 42% performance improvements in human evaluations.
