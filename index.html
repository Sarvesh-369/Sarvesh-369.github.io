About Me

I am an AI Research Assistant working on multimodal AI, vision-language models (VLMs), planning, and reliable agentic systems. I have research experience working with the University of Maryland, College Park (UMD) and the University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC), with publications in LLM personalization, VLM hallucination mitigation, video reasoning, code reasoning, and multimodal evaluation.

My research addresses key safety and robustness issues in state-of-the-art vision and language models. I build embedding-refinement experiments in VLMs to improve visual grounding and reduce hallucinations, construct modular validators and program search loops for classical planning using LLMs, and standardise control flow analysis for control flow deobfuscation in LLMs.

Research & Technical Interests

My technical focus centers on how we can combine natural language reasoning, reinforcement learning, and visual perception to build more robust, safe, and reliable AI systems. I am particularly interested in LLM reasoning traces, vision-language-action (VLA) models, structural program synthesis, and building evaluation benchmarks that test the actual capabilities and limitations of vision models.

Research Interests: Large Language Models (LLMs), Reinforcement Learning, Reasoning & Planning, Robotics, Machine Perception, Computer Vision, Vision-Language-Action Models
